# Wine Wimsey volume/format test (batch dates Nov 9-13, 2026)

From batch date 2026-11-09 (Scott, 2026-11-08), the daily Wine Wimsey batch is 10 feed posts instead of 16 (plus 4 stories), made of 5 plain cards and 5 character posts with no caption and no first comment on any feed post, so the only difference between formats is the image. Queue order alternates plain/character: plain cards take positions 1,3,5,7,9 on odd days of the month and 2,4,6,8,10 on even days. The test replaces the A-D quote/format test (that ended with batch 2026-11-08). It runs for publish days Nov 9-13 at least and is judged on total daily views and earnings vs the Nov 1-7 baseline (about 51K views and $10.65 a day; last 3 days of that baseline about 42K views and $8.25), and on plain-card vs character-post medians per post. Per-post rows are logged in log.csv after each upload.
